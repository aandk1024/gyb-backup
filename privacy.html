Privacy Policy — GYB Backup

Last updated: 2026-09-22

GYB Backup is a personal tool used only by its creator. It reads the creator’s own Gmail messages and settings using read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) and confirms the email address of the account.

Data is stored only on local storage devices managed by the creator. No data is provided, transmitted, or sold to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI.

This app handles no data belonging to anyone other than its creator and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access may be revoked in the Google Account “Third-party apps and services” settings. For inquiries, use this repository’s Issues page.
